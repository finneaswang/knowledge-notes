# Rotational Kinematics
## Core Sentence / 核心句
Rotational kinematics describes orientation through angular position, velocity, and acceleration.
$$\omega=\frac{d\theta}{dt},\qquad\alpha=\frac{d\omega}{dt}$$
$$s=r\theta,\qquad v_t=r\omega,\qquad a_t=r\alpha$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
同一刚体各点的 $\omega$ 相同，但 tangential speed 随 $r$ 改变。
