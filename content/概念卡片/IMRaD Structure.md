# IMRaD Structure

> **IMRaD separates why a study was needed, how it was conducted, what it found, and what those findings mean.**
>
> IMRaD 把研究缘起、执行方法、实际发现和结果意义分开组织。

## Section Logic

- **Introduction**: context, prior work, gap, and research question.
- **Methods**: materials, procedure, analysis, and enough detail to understand or reproduce the study.
- **Results**: observations, measurements, calculations, and patterns with minimal interpretation.
- **Discussion**: explanation, comparison with prior work, implications, limitations, and significance.

Results answers **what happened**; Discussion answers **what it means**. Some papers add a separate Conclusion, while others let Discussion perform that function.
