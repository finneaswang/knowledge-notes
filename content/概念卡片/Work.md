# Work

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Work is energy transferred by the component of force along a displacement.

$$W=\vec F\cdot\Delta\vec r,
\qquad
W=\int\vec F\cdot d\vec r$$

## Watch Out / 易错点

work 是 scalar；只计算沿 displacement 的力分量。$F$-$x$ graph 面积是 work，$F$-$t$ graph 面积是 impulse。
