# Critical Reading

> **Critical reading is a purposeful process of analyzing what a text says, how it is constructed, and what response its choices invite.**
>
> 批判性阅读是带着目的去分析文本说了什么、怎样组织，以及这些写作选择会引导读者作出什么反应。

## Three Phases

~~~text
preview headings and visuals
-> annotate claims, questions, details, and connections
-> restate the main point and why it matters
~~~

## Notes for Gist

A gist note is a short, provisional statement of what matters in a paragraph for the reader's later purpose. It should use the reader's own words, avoid unnecessary copying, and may include a question or response.

Minimal highlighting + one own-word gist note per paragraph is usually more useful than coloring many sentences without processing them.

## Questions to Carry into a Text

- What is the writer's central question or claim?
- What evidence supports it?
- How is that support organized?
- Which terms or assumptions need clarification?
- How does this text connect to or differ from other sources?
- What might I use, question, or extend?
