# Orbital Motion
## Core Sentence / 核心句
Orbital motion combines universal gravitation with circular dynamics, energy, and angular momentum.
$$v_{orb}=\sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}},\qquad E_{circle}=-\frac{GMm}{2r}$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
圆轨道 speed constant；椭圆轨道只有 total energy 与 angular momentum constant，$K$ 和 $U$ 分别变化。
