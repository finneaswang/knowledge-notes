# Circular Motion

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Circular motion requires a net radial force toward the instantaneous center of curvature.

$$a_r=\frac{v^2}{r}=\omega^2r,
\qquad
\sum F_r=m\frac{v^2}{r}$$

## Procedure / 步骤

1. mark the center；2. choose inward positive；3. draw only real forces；4. project them radially；5. add energy if speed changes with height。

## Watch Out / 易错点

centripetal force 不是额外一种力；uniform circular motion 只有 speed 恒定，velocity 仍持续变化。
