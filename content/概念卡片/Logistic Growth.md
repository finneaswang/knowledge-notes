# Logistic Growth

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Logistic growth is growth proportional to both the current population and the fraction of capacity still available.

$$
\frac{dP}{dt}=kP\left(1-\frac PK\right).
$$

$K$ is the carrying capacity. For $0<P<K$, growth is fastest at $P=K/2$, where the solution changes from concave up to concave down.

## Watch Out / 易错点

Carrying capacity comes from the equilibrium value, not from the coefficient in front. A model written $kP(K-P)$ has the same capacity $K$.
