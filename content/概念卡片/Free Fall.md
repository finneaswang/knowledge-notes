# Free Fall

## Core Sentence / 核心句

An object is in free fall whenever gravity is the only significant force, whether it moves upward or downward.

中文理解：自由落体不等于“从静止向下掉”；竖直上抛在离手后同样是 free fall。

## Model / 模型

Near Earth's surface and neglecting air resistance:

$$
\vec a=\vec g,
\qquad
g\approx9.8\ \text{m/s}^2
$$

若 upward positive，则 $a=-g$。

## Watch Out / 易错点

最高点 $v_y=0$，但 $a_y=-g$。$g$ 的大小为正，分量符号由坐标决定。
