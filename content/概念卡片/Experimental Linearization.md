# Experimental Linearization
## Core Sentence / 核心句
Linearization transforms measured variables so a model becomes $Y=mX+b$, allowing physical parameters to be extracted from slope and intercept.
## Procedure / 步骤
1. derive the model；2. choose transformed $X,Y$；3. include transformed units；4. fit a line；5. map slope/intercept back to physics。
## Watch Out / 易错点
交换 axes 会改变 slope；不能换轴后继续沿用原参数关系。
