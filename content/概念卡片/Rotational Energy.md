# Rotational Energy
## Core Sentence / 核心句
A rotating rigid body stores kinetic energy according to its rotational inertia and angular speed.
$$K_{rot}=\frac12I\omega^2,\qquad W=\int\tau,d\theta$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
同时平动和转动时，两部分 kinetic energy 都要计入。
