# AP Physics C Mechanics

## Core Sentence / 核心句

AP Physics C Mechanics uses calculus and a small set of physical principles to model how systems move, interact, exchange energy and momentum, rotate, gravitate, and oscillate.

中文理解：这门课不是一堆公式，而是用微积分、受力与守恒定律，对一个明确系统在某段过程中的运动建模。

## Subject Spine / 学科主干

1. **Kinematics**: [[Position and Displacement]] -> [[Velocity and Speed]] -> [[Acceleration]]
2. **Dynamics**: [[Free-Body Diagrams]] -> [[Newton's Second Law]] -> motion
3. **Energy**: [[Work-Energy Theorem]] + [[Mechanical Energy Conservation]]
4. **Momentum**: [[Impulse]] + [[Conservation of Linear Momentum]]
5. **Rotation**: [[Torque]] -> [[Rotational Inertia]] -> [[Angular Momentum]]
6. **Gravitation**: [[Universal Gravitation]] -> [[Orbital Motion]] -> [[Kepler's Laws]]
7. **Oscillations**: restoring law -> [[Simple Harmonic Motion]] -> [[Resonance]]
8. **Scientific practice**: model -> graph -> [[Experimental Linearization]] -> [[FRQ Scientific Reasoning]]

## Governing Question / 统摄问题

Before choosing an equation, identify the **system boundary**, the **time interval**, the **requested quantity**, and the interaction or conservation law that controls it.

中文抓手：先问“研究谁、看哪段过程、求什么、什么定律真正控制它”，再写方程。

## Watch Out / 易错点

- A formula is valid only under its assumptions; identify the model before substituting numbers.
- Conservation depends on the chosen system and interval; it is not triggered by a keyword alone.
- Energy is scalar, while momentum, force, velocity, torque, and angular momentum require direction or sign conventions.
- Calculus relates local rates and accumulated change; it does not replace the physical law that defines the relationship.
