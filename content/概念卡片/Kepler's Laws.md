# Kepler's Laws
## Core Sentence / 核心句
Kepler's laws describe elliptical orbits, equal areas in equal times, and the period-size relation $T^2\propto a^3$.
$$\frac{dA}{dt}=\frac{L}{2m},\qquad T^2=\frac{4\pi^2}{GM}a^3$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
椭圆使用 semi-major axis $a$；equal-area law 是 angular-momentum conservation 的几何表达。
