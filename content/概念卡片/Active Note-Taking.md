# Active Note-Taking

> **Active note-taking is the selective reconstruction of ideas for a future purpose, not the verbatim capture of words.**
>
> 主动记笔记是为未来任务选择、改写和重组信息，而不是把原话全部复制下来。

## The Processing Loop

~~~text
preview
-> listen/read for concepts and relationships
-> paraphrase and organize
-> mark questions and gaps
-> review and connect
-> retrieve or apply later
~~~

## Choose Structure by Task

| method | useful when |
|---|---|
| Cornell | cues and later self-testing matter |
| Outline | ideas form a clear hierarchy |
| Mapping | relationships matter more than sequence |
| Charting | cases or concepts need comparison |

The best medium is the one that protects attention and produces usable notes. Handwriting can force compression and support diagrams; typing can improve speed and later organization but may invite distraction and transcription.
