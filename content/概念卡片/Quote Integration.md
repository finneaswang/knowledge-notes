# Quote Integration

> **Quote integration embeds selected source language in context, grammar, citation, and analysis so that it advances the writer's argument.**
>
> 引文整合把经过选择的原文放入背景、句法、引用与分析中，使它真正推进作者论证。

## Four-Part Pattern

~~~text
set-up -> grammatical lead-in -> selective quotation + citation -> interpretation
~~~

- Identify the speaker, situation, and relevance.
- Make the quotation fit grammatically into your sentence.
- Quote only the language whose exact wording matters.
- Explain how that language supports, complicates, or changes the claim.

A dropped quotation forces the reader to infer who is speaking, what the passage means, and why it appears. Citation alone does not repair that logical gap.
