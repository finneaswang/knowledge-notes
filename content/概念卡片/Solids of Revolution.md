# Solids of Revolution

## Core Sentence / 核心句

A solid of revolution is measured by rotating a representative slice and accumulating the resulting disk or washer areas.

$$
A_{\text{disk}}=\pi R^2,
\qquad
A_{\text{washer}}=\pi(R^2-r^2).
$$

## Key Rule / 关键规则

Radius is distance to the axis of rotation. For a shifted axis, subtract coordinates; do not automatically use the raw function value.
