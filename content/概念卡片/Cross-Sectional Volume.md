# Cross-Sectional Volume

## Core Sentence / 核心句

A volume with known cross sections accumulates cross-sectional area times infinitesimal thickness.

$$
V=\int_a^bA(x)dx
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
V=\int_c^dA(y)dy.
$$

## Workflow / 做题动作

Draw a perpendicular slice -> find its base length $L$ from the region -> express the cross-section area $A(L)$ -> integrate across the base.

## Watch Out / 易错点

先确认题目给的是 radius、diameter、leg 还是 base。截面垂直于 $x$-axis 通常使用 $dx$；垂直于 $y$-axis 通常使用 $dy$。
