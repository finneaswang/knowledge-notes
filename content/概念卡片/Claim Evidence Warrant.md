# Claim Evidence Warrant

> **A claim states what the reader should accept, evidence supplies support, and a warrant explains why that evidence justifies the claim.**
>
> Claim 提出读者应接受什么，evidence 提供支持，warrant 则解释为什么这些证据能够支持该主张。

## Reasoning Chain

~~~text
claim
-> evidence
-> warrant
-> qualification or alternative explanation
-> implication
~~~

Evidence does not interpret itself. A quotation, statistic, graph, interview, or example becomes part of the writer's argument only after its relevance and meaning are explained.

## Diagnostic Questions

- What exactly does the evidence show?
- Why is it relevant to this claim?
- What assumption connects the two?
- Could the evidence support another explanation?
- What follows if the claim is accepted?

Comments such as `elaborate`, `so what?`, and `implication?` often indicate a missing warrant or consequence, not a need for another quotation.
