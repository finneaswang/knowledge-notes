# Second Derivative

## Core Sentence / 核心句

The second derivative measures how the first derivative, or slope, changes with the input.

中文理解：一阶导数看斜率，二阶导数看斜率怎样变化。

## Formula / 公式或规则

$$
f''(x)=\frac{d}{dx}f'(x)=\frac{d^2f}{dx^2}
$$

## Graph Meaning / 图像含义

$$
f''>0\Rightarrow\text{slope increasing},\qquad
f''<0\Rightarrow\text{slope decreasing}
$$

## Motion Meaning / 运动含义

$$
a(t)=s''(t)
$$

## Watch Out / 易错点

$f''>0$ 不代表 $f$ increasing；它只代表 $f'$ increasing。$f''(c)=0$ 也不能单独证明 inflection point。
