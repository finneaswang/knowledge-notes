# Electric Charge

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Electric charge is the source property that creates electric forces and electric fields.

中文理解：电荷是电力和电场的源头属性，有正负之分，但本身是标量。

## Formula / 公式或规则

Common unit: coulomb (C). Elementary charge magnitude: $e=1.6\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$.

## When To Use / 什么时候用

看到 charged object、positive/negative、source charge，就回到 electric charge。

## Watch Out / 易错点

不要把 charge 的正负号当作 vector direction；force/field 才是 vector。
