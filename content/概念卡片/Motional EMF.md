# Motional EMF

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Motional EMF arises when magnetic force separates charge in a conductor moving through a magnetic field.

中文理解：导体运动使内部载流子具有 bulk velocity，$q\vec v\times\vec B$ 把正负电荷推向不同端点，从而建立 EMF。

## General Expression / 一般表达

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E
=
\int(\vec v\times\vec B)\cdot d\vec\ell
}
$$

simple perpendicular rod：

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E=BLv
}
$$

## Closed Circuit / 闭合回路

$$
\boxed{
I=\frac{BLv}{R}
}
$$

rod magnetic force：

$$
\boxed{
F_B=\frac{B^2L^2v}{R}
}
$$

## Polarity / 极性

positive charge 被 $\vec v\times\vec B$ 推向的一端为 high-potential end。

## Energy / 能量

constant speed：

$$
\boxed{
F_{\text{ext}}v
=
\mathcal EI
=
I^2R
}
$$

## Watch Out / 易错点

- only active field-region length counts。
- open rod has voltage but no steady current。
- EMF is not a mechanical force。
- resistance may vary with rail geometry。
- induced force opposes the driving motion。
