# Charged Ring

## Core Sentence / 核心句

A charged ring is a circular continuous charge distribution.

中文理解：带电圆环通常用对称性抵消横向分量，只留下轴向分量。

## Formula / 公式或规则

$$E_x=\frac{kQx}{(x^2+A^2)^{3/2}}$$

for a uniformly charged ring on its axis.

Its electric potential on the axis is

$$V(x)=\frac{kQ}{\sqrt{x^2+A^2}}.$$

## When To Use / 什么时候用

看到 ring on axis、uniformly charged ring，就先找 symmetry。

## Watch Out / 易错点

难点通常是几何分解，不是积分本身。
