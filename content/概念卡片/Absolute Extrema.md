# Absolute Extrema

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Absolute extrema are the highest and lowest attained outputs on the entire domain or specified interval.

中文理解：绝对最大值和最小值必须与整个指定区间比较，不只是比附近点高或低。

## Closed Interval Method / 闭区间方法

1. Find critical numbers inside $(a,b)$.
2. Add endpoints $a,b$.
3. Evaluate $f$ at every candidate.
4. Compare all outputs.

## Watch Out / 易错点

- local maximum need not be the absolute maximum.
- endpoints may be absolute extrema without being interior critical numbers.
- include nondifferentiable domain points and piecewise joins when relevant.
- [[Extreme Value Theorem]] guarantees existence under its hypotheses but does not calculate the values.
