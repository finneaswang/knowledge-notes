# Rotational Inertia
## Core Sentence / 核心句
Rotational inertia measures how mass is distributed relative to a chosen axis.
$$I=\sum m_ir_i^2,\qquad I=\int r^2,dm$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
$I$ 不只取决于质量和形状，还取决于 axis。
