# Signposting

> **Signposting tells readers how the current move connects to what came before and to the argument's larger route.**
>
> 路标式表达让读者知道当前内容怎样承接前文，以及它位于整条论证路线的什么位置。

## Three Functions

~~~text
look back -> state the current move -> point forward
~~~

A strong topic sentence can summarize its paragraph, bridge from the previous section, and indicate progress relative to the thesis or organizational statement.

## Common Forms

- subordinate clause: compress earlier material before introducing the new emphasis;
- question and answer: turn description into a new analytical problem;
- bridge sentence: connect an earlier condition to a later development;
- pivot: acknowledge one direction, then turn toward the writer's main claim.

Signposting is not the same as filling the essay with `first`, `second`, or `moreover`. Logical relationships should carry the connection whenever possible.
