# Faraday's Law

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Faraday's law states that changing magnetic flux produces EMF, with a direction that opposes the flux change.

中文理解：磁通量变化率决定 induced EMF；负号表示感应效应反抗造成它的磁通变化。

## Law / 定律

single turn：

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E=-\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}
}
$$

$N$ turns：

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E=-N\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}
}
$$

average magnitude：

$$
\boxed{
|\mathcal E_{\text{avg}}|
=
N\left|\frac{\Delta\Phi_B}{\Delta t}\right|
}
$$

## Lenz's Law / 楞次定律

induced magnetic field opposes the **change** in flux：

- increasing flux：oppose increase；
- decreasing flux：support original direction to oppose decrease。

## Flux Changes / 磁通变化渠道

$$
\Phi_B=BA\cos\theta
$$

change $B$、$A$、$\theta$ or field-region overlap。

## Generator / 发电机

rotating $N$-turn coil：

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E(t)=NBA\omega\sin(\omega t)
}
$$

## Watch Out / 易错点

- negative sign cannot be ignored in direction questions。
- multi-turn coil needs $N$。
- oppose flux change, not always external field。
- no motion is required if $B$ changes。
- flux angle uses area normal。
