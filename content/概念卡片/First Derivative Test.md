# First Derivative Test

## Core Sentence / 核心句

The First Derivative Test classifies a critical number by checking how the sign of the derivative changes across it.

中文理解：临界数只是候选；一阶导在左右怎样变号，才决定局部极大、极小或都不是。

## Rule / 规则

$$
f':+\to-\Rightarrow\text{local maximum}
$$

$$
f':-\to+\Rightarrow\text{local minimum}
$$

No sign change means neither.

## Critical-Number Gate / 临界数

$c$ must be in the domain of $f$, with $f'(c)=0$ or $f'(c)$ nonexistent.

## Watch Out / 易错点

- derivative zero alone does not prove an extremum.
- an undefined point outside the function domain is not a critical number.
- even-multiplicity derivative zeros often do not change sign.
