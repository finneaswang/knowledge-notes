# Scholarly Situation

> **A scholarly situation is the combination of subject, purpose, audience, genre, and writer position that determines what an appropriate academic contribution looks like.**
>
> 学术情境是 subject、purpose、audience、genre 与 writer position 的组合；它决定一份学术表达应该选择什么内容、证据、结构和语气。

## The Five Questions

1. **Subject** - What am I writing or speaking about?
2. **Purpose** - What should this contribution accomplish?
3. **Audience** - Who will use or evaluate it, and what do they already know?
4. **Genre** - What recurring form answers this kind of situation?
5. **Writer position** - What authority, responsibility, or limitation do I have here?

## Why It Matters

> **Good writing changes when the situation changes.**

同一个观点写给 friend、professor、engineering client 或 scholarly journal 时，所需背景、术语、证据和语气都会改变。因此 academic writing 不是一套可脱离环境使用的固定句型。
