# Interval of Convergence

## Core Sentence / 核心句

The interval of convergence is the set of x-values where a power series converges.

中文理解：收敛区间回答的是：哪些 $x$ 代进去后这个 power series 会收敛。

## Formula / 公式或规则

Usually found by Ratio Test first, then endpoint testing.

## When To Use / 什么时候用

看到 power series、find interval、endpoint，就找 interval of convergence。

## Watch Out / 易错点

Ratio Test 给的是 open interval；端点必须单独代回原 series 检查。
