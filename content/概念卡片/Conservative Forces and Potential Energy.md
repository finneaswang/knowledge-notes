# Conservative Forces and Potential Energy

## Core Sentence / 核心句

A conservative interaction has path-independent work and can be represented by potential energy.

$$W_{cons}=-\Delta U,
\qquad
F_x=-\frac{dU}{dx}$$

常见例子：gravity、ideal spring force、electrostatic force。

## Watch Out / 易错点

potential energy 属于 interacting system；零点可选，但能量差和物理预测不变。
