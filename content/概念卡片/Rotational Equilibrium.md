# Rotational Equilibrium
## Core Sentence / 核心句
A rigid body is in complete static equilibrium only when both net force and net torque are zero.
$$\sum\vec F=0,\qquad\sum\tau=0$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
translational equilibrium 与 rotational equilibrium 互不自动推出。
