# Inflection Points

## Core Sentence / 核心句

An inflection point is a point on the function where concavity actually changes.

中文理解：拐点不是“二阶导等于零的点”，而是函数凹凸性真正发生转换的定义域内点。

## Candidate Test / 候选检查

Candidates often satisfy

$$
f''(c)=0
\quad\text{or}\quad
f''(c)\text{ DNE}.
$$

Then verify that $f''$ changes sign across $c$.

## Watch Out / 易错点

- $f''(c)=0$ is not sufficient.
- even-multiplicity zeros may preserve the sign.
- the point must belong to the graph of $f$.
