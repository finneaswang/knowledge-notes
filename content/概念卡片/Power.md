# Power

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Power is the rate at which work is done or energy is transferred.

$$P_{avg}=\frac{W}{\Delta t},
\qquad
P=\frac{dW}{dt}=\vec F\cdot\vec v$$

## Watch Out / 易错点

power 描述传递速率，不是能量总量；$Fv$ 只有同向时才可直接按大小相乘。
