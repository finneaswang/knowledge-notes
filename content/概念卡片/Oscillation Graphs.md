# Oscillation Graphs
## Core Sentence / 核心句
In SHM, velocity is the derivative of position and acceleration is opposite and proportional to position.
$$x=A\cos(\omega t+\phi)$$
$$v=-A\omega\sin(\omega t+\phi),\qquad a=-\omega^2x$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
$x=0$ 时 speed 最大而 acceleration 为零；端点恰好相反。
