# Escape Speed
## Core Sentence / 核心句
Escape speed is the minimum launch speed that makes total orbital energy zero.
$$v_{esc}=\sqrt{\frac{2GM}{r}}$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
$r$ 是距天体中心距离；逃逸后重力仍存在，只是轨道不再束缚。
