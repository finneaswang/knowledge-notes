# Radius of Convergence

## Core Sentence / 核心句

The radius of convergence is how far from the center a power series converges.

中文理解：收敛半径表示从中心点往左右多远以内 power series 会收敛。

## Formula / 公式或规则

Find the open interval first, usually by Ratio Test; the radius is half the interval length.

## When To Use / 什么时候用

看到 power series centered at $c$、find radius，就求 radius of convergence。

## Watch Out / 易错点

radius 不包括 endpoint behavior；endpoint belongs to interval of convergence.
