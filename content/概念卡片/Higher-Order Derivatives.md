# Higher-Order Derivatives

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Higher-order derivatives are obtained by repeatedly differentiating, so each order measures how the previous order changes.

中文理解：高阶导数不是全新的运算，而是不断对前一阶导数再求导。

## Formula / 公式或规则

$$
f^{(n)}(x)=\frac{d^nf}{dx^n}
$$

## Interpretation / 直觉

$$
f\to f'\to f''\to f'''\to\cdots
$$

- $f'$：local change of $f$
- $f''$：local change of slope
- $f'''$：local change of the second derivative

## When To Use / 什么时候用

看到 repeated differentiation、$f^{(n)}$、Taylor coefficients、position/velocity/acceleration chain，就想到 higher-order derivatives。

## Watch Out / 易错点

$f^{(n)}$ 表示第 $n$ 阶导数，不是 $f$ 的 $n$ 次幂。
