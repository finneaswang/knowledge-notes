# Rolling Motion
## Core Sentence / 核心句
Rolling without slipping couples translation and rotation through $v_{CM}=R\omega$.
$$v_{CM}=R\omega,\qquad a_{CM}=R\alpha$$
$$K=\frac12Mv_{CM}^2+\frac12I_{CM}\omega^2$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
约束只适用于无滑滚动；friction 方向由接触点滑动趋势决定。
