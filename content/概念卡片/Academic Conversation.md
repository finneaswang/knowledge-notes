# Academic Conversation

> **An academic conversation is an ongoing exchange in which scholars interpret, test, extend, or challenge existing knowledge.**
>
> 学术对话是一场持续进行的交流：学者理解已有观点，再通过证据和推理去检验、延伸或质疑它。

## Two Useful Analogies

### Burke's Parlor

You enter after the discussion has begun. Listen first, identify the argument, then make a relevant contribution. The conversation continues after you leave.

### The Puzzle

Existing scholarship provides pieces already on the table. A useful new piece must connect to them while changing what the larger picture can show.

## What Contribution Means

A contribution may:

- add evidence;
- offer a new interpretation;
- connect previously separate ideas;
- test a claim in another context;
- expose a limitation;
- refine a question or method.

> **Originality is relational: a contribution is new because of how it changes an existing conversation.**
