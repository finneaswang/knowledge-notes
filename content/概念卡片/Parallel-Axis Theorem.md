# Parallel-Axis Theorem
## Core Sentence / 核心句
Moving a rotation axis a perpendicular distance $d$ from a parallel center-of-mass axis increases rotational inertia by $Md^2$.
$$I=I_{CM}+Md^2$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
两轴必须平行，$d$ 是轴间垂距。
