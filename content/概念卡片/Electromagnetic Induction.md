# Electromagnetic Induction

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Electromagnetic induction is the production of EMF by changing magnetic flux.

中文理解：只要 circuit 所关联的 magnetic flux 随时间变化，就会出现 induced EMF；若 circuit closed，还会出现 induced current。

## Core Law / 核心定律

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal E=-N\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}
}
$$

$$
\Phi_B=\int\vec B\cdot d\vec A
$$

## How Flux Can Change / 磁通如何变化

- magnetic field magnitude changes；
- loop area changes；
- loop orientation changes；
- conductor enters or leaves a field；
- field distribution changes；
- conductor moves through a field。

## Two Views / 两种视角

- microscopic: [[Lorentz Force]] separates charges in a moving conductor；
- macroscopic: [[Faraday's Law]] relates circuit EMF to flux change。

## Energy / 能量

induced current 的 magnetic effect opposes the change producing it。mechanical or field-source work supplies the electrical energy。

## Watch Out / 易错点

- electrostatic [[Induction]] 与 electromagnetic induction 是不同概念。
- motion is sufficient in many cases, not necessary。
- flux change, not field alone, controls induction。
- direction follows Lenz's law。
