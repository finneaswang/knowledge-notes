# Velocity and Speed

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Velocity is the rate of change of position; instantaneous speed is the magnitude of instantaneous velocity.

中文理解：velocity 带方向，speed 只保留大小。

## Formula / 公式

$$
\vec v_{\text{avg}}=\frac{\Delta\vec r}{\Delta t},
\qquad
\vec v=\frac{d\vec r}{dt},
\qquad
\text{speed}=|\vec v|
$$

$$
\text{average speed}=\frac{\text{total distance}}{\Delta t}
$$

## Watch Out / 易错点

Instantaneous speed $=|v|$，但 average speed 通常不等于 $|v_{\text{avg}}|$。
