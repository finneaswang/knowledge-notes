# Pendulums
## Core Sentence / 核心句
Pendulum motion becomes approximately simple harmonic when a small-angle restoring torque is proportional to angular displacement.
$$T_{simple}=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$$
$$T_{physical}=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{I_{pivot}}{Mgd}}$$
$$T_{torsional}=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{I}{\kappa}}$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
simple/physical pendulum 的 SHM 结论依赖 small-angle approximation；$I$ 必须对应 pivot。
