# Simple Harmonic Motion
## Core Sentence / 核心句
Simple harmonic motion occurs when the restoring force is proportional and opposite to displacement from equilibrium.
$$F=-kx,\qquad a=-\omega^2x$$
$$f=\frac1T,\qquad\omega=2\pi f$$
## Watch Out / 易错点
周期运动不一定是 SHM；angular frequency 不是刚体 angular velocity。
