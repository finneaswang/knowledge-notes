# Mechanical Energy Conservation

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Mechanical energy is conserved when the chosen system has no unaccounted nonconservative energy transfer across its boundary.

$$E_{mech}=K+U,
\qquad
K_i+U_i=K_f+U_f$$

中文理解：总能量始终守恒，但机械能守恒有条件；摩擦可能把机械能转为 thermal/internal energy。

## Watch Out / 易错点

先画 [[System Boundary]]；不要同时把同一 conservative interaction 写成 work 和 $\Delta U$。
