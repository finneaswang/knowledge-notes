# Arc Length

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Arc length accumulates tiny straight-line approximations obtained from the Pythagorean theorem.

$$
dL=\sqrt{(dx)^2+(dy)^2}.
$$

$$
L=\int_a^b\sqrt{1+[f'(x)]^2}dx
$$

and parametrically

$$
L=\int_a^b\sqrt{[x'(t)]^2+[y'(t)]^2}dt.
$$

## Connection / 连接

Parametric arc length is also distance traveled, because $\sqrt{[x']^2+[y']^2}$ is speed.
