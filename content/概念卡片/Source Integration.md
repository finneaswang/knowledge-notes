# Source Integration

> **Source integration connects an accurately represented source to the writer's own reasoning, purpose, and contribution.**
>
> 来源整合是把被准确表达的来源，连接到作者自己的推理、目的与贡献中。

## Minimal Move

~~~text
They say -> I say -> So what
~~~

- **They say**: name the scholar and accurately quote, summarize, or [[Acceptable Paraphrase|paraphrase]] the relevant claim.
- **I say**: state whether you agree, extend, complicate, apply, or challenge it.
- **So what**: explain why this relationship matters to the question or audience.

A citation alone does not integrate a source. The reader must be able to see why the source appears at that point and how it changes the writer's reasoning.
