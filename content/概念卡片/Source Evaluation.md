# Source Evaluation

> **Source evaluation asks whether a source is credible, traceable, current enough, and relevant for this particular scholarly purpose.**
>
> 来源评估同时判断它是否值得信、能否追溯、时效是否足够，以及是否适合当前任务。

## Evaluation Dimensions

- **Authority**: author expertise and institutional context
- **Publication**: publisher, editorial process, and peer review
- **Purpose**: research, explanation, advocacy, sales, or entertainment
- **Evidence**: methods, data, references, and traceability
- **Currency**: whether age matters for the question
- **Relevance**: fit with the exact claim, audience, genre, and assignment

## Central Distinction

> **Reliability is not the same as relevance.**

A reputable news report may be reliable but too general for a technical claim. A rigorous article may be credible but answer a different question. Evaluation therefore ends with a use decision, not merely a quality label.
