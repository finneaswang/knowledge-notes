# Center of Mass

## Core Sentence / 核心句

The center of mass is the mass-weighted average position of a system.

$$\vec r_{CM}=\frac{\sum m_i\vec r_i}{\sum m_i},
\qquad
\vec r_{CM}=\frac1M\int\vec r\,dm$$

## Watch Out / 易错点

质心不必落在物体材料内部；连续体积分时先按线、面、体密度写出 $dm$。
