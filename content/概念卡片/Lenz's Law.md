# Lenz's Law

## Core Sentence / 核心句

The induced current creates a magnetic field that opposes the **change** in magnetic flux.

中文理解：感应效应反抗的是磁通量的变化，不是永远反向于原 magnetic field。

## Direction Logic / 方向逻辑

- flux increasing：induced $\vec B$ opposes the original flux direction；
- flux decreasing：induced $\vec B$ supports the original flux direction to resist the decrease。

## Stable Procedure / 固定步骤

1. choose a viewing direction / surface normal；
2. identify external flux direction；
3. decide increasing or decreasing；
4. choose induced $\vec B$ to oppose that change；
5. use right-hand grip rule to obtain current direction。

## Why It Must Oppose / 为什么必须反抗

如果 induced effect 增强导致它自身的变化，系统会无外界做功地自发放大 current 与 energy，违反 energy conservation。Lenz direction ensures mechanical or source work supplies the induced electrical energy。

## Deep Phrase / 记忆句

> **Faraday gives the amount; Lenz gives the direction.**
>
> 法拉第定大小，楞次定方向。

## Watch Out / 易错点

- oppose the flux **change**, not simply the field；
- current direction comes only after induced-field direction；
- entering and leaving a field give opposite currents；
- a loop moving entirely inside a uniform field may have no net EMF because flux is unchanged。
