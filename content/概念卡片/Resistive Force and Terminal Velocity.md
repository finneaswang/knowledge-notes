# Resistive Force and Terminal Velocity

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Terminal velocity is reached asymptotically when speed-dependent resistance balances the driving force, making acceleration zero while velocity remains nonzero.

线性阻力下落：

$$m\frac{dv}{dt}=mg-bv,
\qquad v_t=\frac{mg}{b}$$

从静止释放：

$$v(t)=v_t(1-e^{-bt/m})$$

## Watch Out / 易错点

terminal velocity 不是停止；正负号取决于坐标方向，阻力始终反对相对流体速度。
