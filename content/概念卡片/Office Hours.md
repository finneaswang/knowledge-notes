# Office Hours

> **Office hours are scheduled opportunities to ask an instructor or TA specific questions, develop ideas, and interpret feedback.**
>
> Office hours 是与教师或 TA 进行具体学术问答、发展想法和理解反馈的固定时间。

## A Useful Question Pattern

1. Give the relevant context.
2. Explain what you currently understand or attempted.
3. Identify the exact point of uncertainty.
4. Ask what reasoning, resource, or next step would help.

Office hours are not reserved for crises or students who are already confident. Preparing a specific question lets the instructor diagnose the gap instead of guessing what “I do not understand” means.
