# Potential Energy Curves

## Core Sentence / 核心句

A potential-energy curve encodes force in its negative slope and kinetic energy in the gap below the total-energy line.

$$F_x=-\frac{dU}{dx},
\qquad
K=E-U\ge0$$

## Reading Rules / 读图规则

- $U\le E$: allowed region；
- $U=E$: turning point；
- local minimum: stable equilibrium；
- local maximum: unstable equilibrium。

## Watch Out / 易错点

turning point 只说明瞬时 $v=0$；若 slope 不为零，它不是 equilibrium。
