# Citation Styles

> **A citation style is a standardized system for showing source use and directing readers to complete source information.**
>
> 引用格式是一套标准化系统，用来标明来源使用，并把读者带到完整来源信息。

## Functional Comparison

| Style | In-text or note logic | End list |
|---|---|---|
| APA | author-date | References |
| MLA | author/title key plus location when relevant | Works Cited |
| Chicago Notes and Bibliography | superscript note number, then full or shortened note | Bibliography |

The instructor, assignment, journal, or required manual determines the style and edition. Discipline associations are tendencies, not permission to choose freely.

## Reliable Workflow

1. Identify the exact required style and edition.
2. Mark every quotation, paraphrase, data point, and borrowed interpretation.
3. Make the citation's scope unambiguous.
4. Match each citation or note to a complete entry.
5. Verify generated metadata and formatting against the required guide.

## Boundary

- APA 7 book references omit publisher location.
- APA journal article titles are not italicized; journal title and volume are italicized.
- Chicago currently prefers shortened notes over routine `ibid.` use.
- Author-count and locator rules vary by style and source type.
