# One-Dimensional Motion

## Core Sentence / 核心句

In one-dimensional motion, velocity is the derivative of position, acceleration is the derivative of velocity, and speed is the magnitude of velocity.

中文理解：一维运动中，位置求导得到带方向的速度，速度再求导得到加速度；速率只保留速度大小。

## Formula / 公式

$$
v(t)=x'(t),
\qquad
a(t)=v'(t)=x''(t),
\qquad
\text{speed}=|v(t)|.
$$

## Sign Map / 符号判断

- $v>0$: moving right
- $v<0$: moving left
- $a>0$: velocity increasing
- $a<0$: velocity decreasing
- $va>0$: speed increasing
- $va<0$: speed decreasing

## Watch Out / 易错点

- Velocity decreasing is not the same as speed decreasing.
- A particle may have $v=0$ while $a\neq0$.
- On a velocity graph, height gives direction and slope gives acceleration.
- At $v=0$, inspect nearby behavior before applying the sign shortcut.
