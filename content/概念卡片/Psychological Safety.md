# Psychological Safety

> **Psychological safety is confidence that one can ask questions, admit uncertainty, test ideas, or disagree without humiliation.**
>
> 心理安全感是相信自己可以提问、承认不确定、尝试想法或表达分歧，而不会因此被羞辱。

It does not remove standards or disagreement. It makes unfinished reasoning visible enough to receive evidence, questions, correction, and feedback.

## Observable Practices

- listen before responding;
- paraphrase to check understanding;
- challenge ideas rather than people;
- respond specifically and constructively;
- admit uncertainty and identify how to verify;
- make room for different participation styles and voices.
