# Induction

## Core Sentence / 核心句

Induction is charge redistribution caused by a nearby charged object without direct contact.

中文理解：感应带电是不接触也能让导体内部电荷重新分布。

## Formula / 公式或规则

No core equation here; it is a qualitative electrostatics mechanism.

## When To Use / 什么时候用

看到 charged object near neutral conductor、electroscope leaves spread，就想到 induction。

## Watch Out / 易错点

induction 不等于摩擦起电；它强调不接触。
