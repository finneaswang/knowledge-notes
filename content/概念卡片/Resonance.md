# Resonance
## Core Sentence / 核心句
Resonance is the large steady-state response produced when a driving frequency approaches a system's natural frequency.
## Watch Out / 易错点
共振峰对应 amplitude 最大，不是 period 最大；damping 限制峰值并改变峰宽。
